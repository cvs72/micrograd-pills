# Design pass plan (draft for the owner's approval; becomes loop/PLAN.md when the loop runs)

Scope: the interface of the twelve pills and the index: navigation, credits, the phone layout, and one leftover
of the kit pass. NOT the content: every title, step text, ledger line, eyebrow, on-stage label and number stays
exactly as it is, and the loop proves that mechanically on every iteration (readable-content dumps identical to
`loop/baseline/`). Pixels are allowed to change in this pass, so the gate is different from the kit pass: the
pages are captured at desktop and phone size, an independent reviewer with vision reads every capture of the
row's pages against the row's description, and the static checks named in each row must pass.

Rows are worked top to bottom, one per iteration. The builder ticks a row only when its own checks pass; the loop
re-verifies, the reviewer judges the captures and the diff, and the loop records the verdict. Editing anything in
this file other than the tick boxes stops the loop.

VIEWPORTS: 1440x810, 1024x768, 844x390, 390x844   (desktop, tablet, phone landscape, phone portrait; the gate captures all four)
SHOT_TOL: none (pixel differences are reported, not judged, in this pass)
MAX_REVIEW_REJECTS: 4

- [ ] D01 | fit | Deterministic first frame. The kit lays out and fits the camera once the fonts are ready (`document.fonts.ready`, plus a re-layout on `loadingdone` for fonts requested later), so step 1 is framed like every later step. Remove the `firstFitBodyFallback` switch from the kit and from pill 05. Checks: fit, content identical, desktop captures within 0.01 of the baseline on every step except step 1 (reported).
- [ ] D02 | menu | A menu button on every pill and on the index, top right, the standard three-line icon with the label "Menu" for assistive technology. It opens a panel listing the twelve pills by number and name with the current one marked, plus "Home"; it closes on Escape, on a click outside, and after following a link; focus moves into the panel when it opens and returns to the button when it closes; `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` are set; it works with the keyboard alone and at every viewport width. The step counter ("1 / 7") moves left of the button. The hidden K05 footer links are removed (the menu replaces them). Checks: menu, html-validate, content identical, captures at all four sizes.
- [ ] D03 | footer | A visible footer line on every page, fixed at the bottom, one line, small, in the muted colour: "Developed by cvs72 using Claude · MIT License · Contact". Hover or keyboard focus on `cvs72` shows a bubble "Enjoy, Carlos Val Souto."; hover or focus on "Contact" shows a bubble "Contact the developer", and that bubble is the link, to `https://github.com/cvs72/micrograd-pills/issues/new`; on touch screens the first tap opens the bubble and the second follows the link. "MIT License" links to `LICENSE` copied into `site/` by `build.js`. The handle is always lowercase `cvs72`. The kit's `layout()` subtracts the footer's height so the stage never hides behind it. Checks: footer, links, content identical, captures at all four sizes.
- [ ] D04 | responsive | Every page renders correctly at every screen size from 320 px to 2560 px wide, in both orientations, and re-lays itself out live when the window is resized or the phone is rotated (no reload). Fluid rules, not fixed pixel positions: the stage and deck share the viewport by proportion, text sizes scale with `clamp()`, nothing is cut off, no horizontal scroll at any width, labels stay legible (minimum 11 px) and inside the stage, the camera fit uses the narrow padding under 640 px so the scene fills the width. Phone portrait (under 700 px wide): the stage on top and the deck below, deck text at least 16 px, ledger below the text, the three buttons at least 44 px tall and reachable with a thumb, the dots row under them, the stage at least 45% of the height. Phone landscape (short viewports, under 500 px high): the deck beside the stage or collapsed to the title and one line with the text reachable by scrolling the deck, never the page. Tablet: the desktop arrangement with the deck's columns stacking when the width is under 900 px. Large monitors: the deck's text column capped at a readable measure (about 70 characters), the stage using the rest. Menu and footer usable at every size. The 1440x810 layout does not change. Checks: responsive, content identical, captures at all four sizes, 1440x810 captures within 0.01 of the previous iteration's.
- [ ] D05 | polish | The index: the same menu and footer, the lead paragraph, the list as cards that are easy to tap on a phone (already), a "Start with 01" link under the lead. Every pill's `<title>` and description checked once more against the index names. Checks: links, skeleton, html-validate, content identical, captures at all four sizes.

## Decisions taken in this plan (change them before the loop runs, not during)
- The baseline for this pass is re-captured by the owner from `main` before the loop starts, at both viewports, and it is the content fingerprint that is frozen; the pixel comparison runs only as a report for the reviewer (worst difference per pill), except where a row says "within 0.01".
- The `responsive` check is mechanical as well as visual: Playwright loads every page at 320, 390, 600, 768, 1024, 1280, 1920 and 2560 px wide (and at 844x390), asserts no horizontal overflow, every control inside the viewport, no element of the deck clipped, then resizes the window from desktop to phone and back without reload and asserts the same again.
- The reviewer sees, for each ticked row, the captures of the row's pages at all four sizes and the diff of the sources, and judges against the row's text; a visible defect (clipped text, overlapping labels, a control off-screen, unreadable size) is blocking.
- When the pass is done, the owner approves the look and the captures become the new `loop/baseline/` for the next content loop (Part 2).
- Out of scope: the video versions (16:9 and portrait renders), the Spanish version, and any change to the stage scenes themselves.
